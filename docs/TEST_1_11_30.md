Full release uses the existing per-feature ledgers plus FULL_RELEASE_SCOPE_ACCEPTANCE.json. Null means evidence is outstanding. Each completed row names the firmware, exact candidate fingerprint and dated hardware observations for its own model, role and topology. Three-phase also requires the firmware in the qualified profile registry. The community trial profile does not qualify. Supported Homey platforms can use separate battery registers starting at OS 12.4.5. The upstream MIT notice ships in the archive.
