## What changed and why

- House-only planning/control no longer depends on disabled office telemetry.
  Whole-property CT1 demand retains office net imports rather than subtracting them.
  Missing-hour fallback was explicitly converted from 0.50 + 0.15 to 0.65 kW;
  that is a provisional baseline, not measured future demand. Solar credit is zero.
- Backward peak preservation carries both readiness stock and earlier higher-price
  demand through planned replenishment. It can serve a profitable morning period,
  refill, then preserve the 95% readiness target for the evening peak.
- Exact house member identities are enrolled privately. System power is consumed
  once; physical watt tiles are not summed. If aggregate SOC becomes stale, the
  minimum fresh exact-member SOC is a labelled planning lower bound, not an
  averaged aggregate, replacement capability or new Energy contributor.
- Reserve changes and native restoration address every enrolled physical member
  that needs a change. Unexpected manual targets are preserved. Verification waits
  for sixteen agreeing half-second polls so a delayed pre-write status reply does
  not immediately hand an unsettled transition to the watchdog. Reads may refresh;
  an uncertain reserve SET is not blindly replayed.
- Watchdog restoration, stopping progress and busy replies have explicit ownership
  handling. Only identity-bound, recent, verified native completion can reconcile
  a runner expectation; arbitrary manual changes or unresolved faults still block.
- Office depletion/readiness accounting and configurable installed/planned house
  capacity have regression coverage; disabled office controls remain disabled.
