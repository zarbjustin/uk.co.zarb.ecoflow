Connect supported EcoFlow STREAM batteries, solar devices and Smart Meters to Homey Pro. Readings, Insights, widgets and Homey Energy depend on the model and device role. Pair one original STREAM Home Battery per installation for battery Energy and widgets; optional unit monitors do not add another battery to Energy. Widget capacity supports local estimates, not Homey Energy capacity configuration. Original STREAM uses Developer API keys and offers guarded settings actions and Flows; actual charging and export still depend on EcoFlow modes, schedules and limits. STREAM 5000 (ES21) and AC 5000 (ES22) use captured app protocols through your EcoFlow app sign-in, with no control commands. Physical monitoring needs no opt-in and contributes no battery Energy. Aggregate Energy pairing has separate default-off consent and remains under model-specific hardware validation. An optional Developer API fallback supplies system battery level only. Service changes can affect availability. Expansion batteries, Gateway and STREAM 3000 cannot be paired; separate installations are not combined automatically.

Use the original Smart Meter or Dual CT as a property Energy source only at the whole-property incomer, avoiding overlapping meters. Their imported/exported totals are locally calculated estimates from accepted grid-power readings; unseen gaps are not filled. Dual CT model 421/firmware 1.0.0.41 defaults to single-phase CT1; three-phase CT1 is an explicitly consented community trial with hardware validation still open. Optional CT2 is excluded from property Energy and can contain battery as well as solar exchange. Display settings do not change the paired accounting mode. Keep existing devices to preserve Flows and history. This app checks overlapping EcoFlow meters only; review other apps’ meters in Homey Energy yourself. Whole-Homey API access is not requested. Advanced cross-app coordination needs a separately configured owner automation; previous in-app cross-app monitoring cannot start. Saved selections and Flow card identities are retained. Support and advanced diagnostics are in app settings. Reports remain local until copied or downloaded; review them before sharing because schedules and energy patterns can reveal household activity. This is an independent integration, not an official EcoFlow or Athom app.
