Your EcoFlow STREAM turns a sunny balcony into a real home power station, and this app brings all of it into Homey. Watch solar generation, battery charge, and grid import and export update live on the Homey Energy dashboard, with every reading saved to Insights so you can see exactly where your energy goes.

From there, your home can act on its own power. Charge the battery while the sun is strong, save it for the evening peak, switch operating modes, control each AC output, and get alerted the moment something needs attention, all through Homey Flows, with no app-hopping required.

Pair STREAM Home Battery for one complete installation and use it for Homey Energy and widgets. A Physical STREAM Unit is one inverter or battery and does not provide whole-system totals. Optionally enter the installation capacity in the Home Battery settings to show stored and usable kWh and a clearly labelled runtime estimate.

STREAM 5000 support is an opt-in, read-only beta because EcoFlow provides no supported public API for these products. Enable beta pairing in the app settings, then use STREAM Home Battery (5000 Beta) once per verified 5 kWh-family installation for Homey Energy. STREAM 5000 Series Unit (Beta) is an optional physical monitor with charged/discharged meters; exclude it from Energy when both roles are paired to avoid duplicate totals. The current adapter supports STREAM AC 5000 (ES22). The device model, capabilities and pairing may change, and re-pairing may be required, when EcoFlow releases its official API. Additional 5000-series products remain unavailable until their serial prefixes and telemetry are verified.
