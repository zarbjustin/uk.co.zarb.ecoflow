Your EcoFlow STREAM turns a sunny balcony into a real home power station, and this app brings all of it into Homey. Watch solar generation, battery charge, and grid import and export update live on the Homey Energy dashboard, with every reading saved to Insights so you can see exactly where your energy goes.

From there, your home can act on its own power. Charge the battery while the sun is strong, save it for the evening peak, switch operating modes, control each AC output, and get alerted the moment something needs attention, all through Homey Flows, with no app-hopping required.

Pair STREAM Home Battery for one complete installation and use it for Homey Energy and widgets. A STREAM Unit is one inverter or battery and does not provide whole-system totals. Optionally enter the installation capacity in the Home Battery settings to show stored and usable kWh and a clearly labelled runtime estimate.

STREAM 5000 support is an opt-in, read-only beta using EcoFlow's app connection rather than a verified official Developer-key API. Enable beta pairing in this Homey app's settings, then use STREAM Home Battery (5000 Beta) once per installation for Homey Energy. STREAM 5000 Series Unit (Beta) is an optional physical telemetry monitor only; all Homey Energy power and charged/discharged totals come from the Home Battery device. Test supports core monitoring for STREAM AC 5000 (ES22) and STREAM 5000 (ES21); hardware validation remains open for each model. No 5000 control commands are sent. Models, capabilities and pairing may change as official API support becomes clearer. Expansion, Gateway and STREAM 3000 pairing remain unavailable pending model-specific evidence. Mixed-generation installations are not automatically merged.
