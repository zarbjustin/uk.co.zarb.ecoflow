STREAM 5000 Series (Beta)

Experimental, read-only integration
EcoFlow does not currently provide a supported public API for these products. This beta uses the connection used by EcoFlow's mobile app. It may stop working, and the device model, capabilities, Homey Energy behaviour, credentials or pairing may need to change when EcoFlow releases its official API. Re-pairing may be required.

Disabled by default. Turning this off later prevents new pairing only; it does not remove or disable STREAM 5000 devices you already paired.

Use these settings in the Homey EcoFlow integration, not in EcoFlow's own mobile app.

  1. Install a Homey app version that includes the beta choices. They are currently available in the Homey test version.
  2. Enable beta pairing above, then open Add device → EcoFlow STREAM Series.
  3. Choose STREAM Home Battery (5000) once for the installation's Homey Energy readings.
  4. Optionally choose STREAM 5000 Series Unit for a physical monitor, not a second Energy battery.

This switch permits pairing only; it does not install or update the app or dynamically add device types. If the two beta choices are missing, report the installed EcoFlow Homey app version, Homey OS version, Homey mobile-app version and a screenshot of the device picker. Please do not delete existing devices until the correct replacement is visible; deleting a device can affect its Flows and history.

Verified STREAM 5000-family units use EcoFlow's app connection because they are not available through the supported public API. This beta supports STREAM AC 5000 (ES22…) and STREAM 5000 (ES21…) core battery monitoring. ES21 still needs Homey hardware validation; direct PV/string tiles, expansion batteries, STREAM 3000 and Gateway pairing are not enabled. Your EcoFlow account sign-in is stored separately from the keys above. Monitoring only — controls are intentionally disabled and no commands are sent. Removing the last STREAM 5000-family unit also clears it.

Setup and Energy diagnostics

This local report shows the installed app and Homey versions, packaged/registered battery drivers, beta pairing access, anonymous Energy accounting counters and observed battery peer counts and which peer fields have been seen during this session. After opening an existing beta pairing device list, it also includes a cached account inventory: model prefixes, counts and unverified product-category hints for expansion batteries, STREAM 3000, Gateway and Dual CT. You can cancel pairing without adding or deleting a device. The inventory is not an installation membership list; a missing expansion entry does not mean no expansion is connected. Possible overlap between Home Batteries is a review hint, not confirmed membership; this report does not combine or change devices. It does not contact EcoFlow or include credentials, device names or complete serial numbers. Only four-character product prefixes are shown. Inventory observations reset on account changes or app restart. The report also explains why 5000 controls remain disabled, flags fresh mixed-family peer observations for review, and lists the evidence still needed for new product categories. A recent setting receipt is not proof that a command changed physical power. Copy it when reporting missing device choices or incorrect energy totals. Also include your Homey mobile-app version and a screenshot; this report cannot determine the mobile device picker or store channel.

Dual CT research — opt-in, read-only

Two-minute read-only discovery using saved accounts. Default capture records structure only. Local numeric candidates are optional; sharing them requires additional consent. Energy, Flows and commands remain disabled.

How capture works and privacy

This is not Dual CT pairing or meter support. Start checks the saved EcoFlow app and Developer accounts once each, then listens for up to two minutes to at most two owned devices explicitly labelled Smart Meter Dual CT in product metadata, or the provisionally observed app productType 421. A display name alone never qualifies; shared devices are excluded. ProductType 421 is admitted for discovery only, not verified readings or pairing. It uses existing shared MQTT connections, which may affect an EcoFlow app session. Developer observations require the device to appear in that account's inventory. At most three read-only operations per identified meter check its quota and resolved host, using the existing request retry policy; hosts must also be known STREAM models in the same Developer inventory. A resolved host does not prove CT channel membership. No commands, verified channel readings, Energy sources or Flows are enabled. By default only anonymous frame structure and counts and allowlisted JSON key/types are retained in memory; no values, raw payloads, device names or complete serials are saved. The report separates empty, unavailable and observed responses; observed telemetry does not establish units, direction or channel roles. The separate optional numeric preview retains unverified candidates in memory only, never in normal support exports. A separate candidate export requires explicit consent and a manual Copy or Download action. It requires valid model/message headers and a supported encoding interpretation. An omitted marker is tested as an unverified zero/default candidate only after strict payload checks; masked or unsupported markers remain blocked. Additional scalar fields have no assigned units or channel roles. No reports are sent automatically. Afterwards use Show integration support report at your convenience. No detected model or no frames is inconclusive: this path may not expose the meter directly.

Let the capture run, then send one integration report when convenient. No timed screenshots are required.
