Homey Energy

Use one whole-property grid meter in Homey Energy. A meter on a downstream circuit must not also count as the whole-property meter. Review each meter’s Energy settings before changing sources.

Add one Home Battery per separate installation. Physical unit monitors provide device readings without contributing a second battery to Homey Energy. Capacity entered for app widgets does not set Homey Energy capacity.

Dual CT monitoring is available; its CT1 property Energy contribution is still being validated. STREAM 5000 battery monitoring is available; installation-level Homey Energy accounting is still being validated. Battery settings are managed in EcoFlow.

Off by default while installation Energy validation is in progress. This permits adding a new installation-level Home Battery only. Turning it off does not remove existing devices or stop their accounting. Check for overlap before adding a battery.

Support and advanced diagnostics

Open a local support report or use an optional diagnostic tool when investigating a problem. Reports are never sent automatically. Review exported information before sharing.

Connection details and supported devices

STREAM 5000 (ES21) and STREAM AC 5000 (ES22) monitoring uses EcoFlow’s app service rather than its Developer API. Service changes can affect availability. Monitoring does not send control commands. Expansion batteries, STREAM 3000 and Gateway pairing are not available. Readings depend on model, configuration and fresh data.

Missing a device choice? Check the installed app and Homey versions before changing devices.

Integration report

This local report shows the installed app and Homey versions, packaged/registered battery drivers, separate aggregate pairing access, anonymous Energy accounting counters and observed battery peer counts and which peer fields have been seen during this session. After opening an existing app-connected pairing device list, it also includes a cached account inventory: model prefixes, counts and unverified product-category hints for expansion batteries, STREAM 3000, Gateway and Dual CT. You can cancel pairing without adding or deleting a device. The inventory is not an installation membership list; a missing expansion entry does not mean no expansion is connected. Possible overlap between Home Batteries is a review hint, not confirmed membership; this report does not combine or change devices. It does not contact EcoFlow or include credentials, device names or complete serial numbers. Only four-character product prefixes are shown. Inventory observations reset on account changes or app restart. The report also explains why 5000 controls remain disabled, flags fresh mixed-family peer observations for review, and lists the evidence still needed for new product categories. A recent setting receipt is not proof that a command changed physical power. Copy it when reporting missing device choices or incorrect energy totals. Also include your Homey mobile-app version and a screenshot; this report cannot determine the mobile device picker or store channel.

Dual CT research — opt-in, read-only

Two-minute read-only discovery using saved accounts. Default capture records structure only. Local numeric candidates are optional; sharing them requires additional consent. Energy, Flows and commands remain disabled.

How capture works and privacy

This is not Dual CT pairing or meter support. Start checks the saved EcoFlow app and Developer accounts once each, then listens for up to two minutes to at most two owned devices explicitly labelled Smart Meter Dual CT in product metadata, or the provisionally observed app productType 421. A display name alone never qualifies; shared devices are excluded. ProductType 421 is admitted for discovery only, not verified readings or pairing. It uses existing shared MQTT connections, which may affect an EcoFlow app session. Developer observations require the device to appear in that account's inventory. At most three read-only operations per identified meter check its quota and resolved host, using the existing request retry policy; hosts must also be known STREAM models in the same Developer inventory. A resolved host does not prove CT channel membership. No commands, verified channel readings, Energy sources or Flows are enabled. By default only anonymous frame structure and counts and allowlisted JSON key/types are retained in memory; no values, raw payloads, device names or complete serials are saved. The report separates empty, unavailable and observed responses; observed telemetry does not establish units, direction or channel roles. The separate optional numeric preview retains unverified candidates in memory only, never in normal support exports. A separate candidate export requires explicit consent and a manual Copy or Download action. It requires valid model/message headers and a supported encoding interpretation. An omitted marker is tested as an unverified zero/default candidate only after strict payload checks; masked or unsupported markers remain blocked. Additional scalar fields have no assigned units or channel roles. No reports are sent automatically. Afterwards use Show integration support report at your convenience. No detected model or no frames is inconclusive: this path may not expose the meter directly.
