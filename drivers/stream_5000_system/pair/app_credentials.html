STREAM 5000 Home Battery Beta

Read this first Supported STREAM 5000 installations are not available through EcoFlow's public API, so this beta Home Battery is monitored through EcoFlow's app connection. That requires signing in with your EcoFlow account password. The implementation may change when EcoFlow releases its official API, including requiring re-pairing. Your email and password are stored in this app's settings on your Homey and are only sent to EcoFlow's own servers. Monitoring only — controls are intentionally disabled and no commands are sent.

Use the same email address and password you use in the EcoFlow mobile app. This is separate from the Access Key / Secret Key used by the Developer-API devices in this app. Each verified installation is offered once as the Homey Energy Home Battery; physical units can be paired separately as monitors.
