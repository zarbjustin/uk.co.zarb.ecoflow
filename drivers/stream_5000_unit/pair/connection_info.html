STREAM 5000 Series Unit

Read-only monitoring for supported STREAM 5000 (ES21) and STREAM AC 5000 (ES22) units.

Sign in with your EcoFlow app account. This connection uses EcoFlow's app service, not Developer API keys, and its availability can change with EcoFlow updates.

No Energy opt-in is needed for this unit monitor. It does not contribute to Homey Energy or send control commands. Where supplied, system readings are labelled separately from unit readings; unknown readings stay unavailable.

STREAM Home Battery (5000) is a separate installation-level Energy device with its own pairing opt-in. Do not replace or delete an existing device to add this monitor.
