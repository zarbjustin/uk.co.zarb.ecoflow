STREAM 5000 Unit Monitor

Choose an individual STREAM 5000 (ES21) or STREAM AC 5000 (ES22) to see its own readings. This monitor is optional and adds no battery to Homey Energy.

For battery storage in Homey Energy, choose STREAM 5000 Home Battery. For panel generation, choose STREAM 5000 Solar.

Sign in with your EcoFlow app account. Manage battery settings in EcoFlow.
