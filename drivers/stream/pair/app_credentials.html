STREAM 5000 app connection Verified STREAM 5000 products are not available through EcoFlow's supported public API. Homey therefore signs in through EcoFlow's app connection and reads telemetry only. Controls are intentionally disabled and no commands are sent. This connection may change without notice.

Use the same email address and password as the EcoFlow mobile app. They are stored in this app's settings on your Homey and sent only to EcoFlow's own servers. The resulting device is the same STREAM Home Battery used by every other STREAM installation.
